# TXOP admission for Normal Ack and Basic Block Ack

The prerequisite series supplies aggregate transmission (#1364) and protected Basic opening, continuation and feedback recovery (#1503). This series covers ordinary unicast QoS DATA. HCF checks the whole exchange before the data service consumes its queued sources. A fresh positive grant can require a smaller MPDU even when the MSDU fits the configured fragmentation threshold.

1. Give the fragmentation policy a complete MPDU length bound. The result distinguishes an ordinary frame, a legal partition and an impossible partition. IEEE Std 802.11-2024 clause 10.4 limits the partition to sixteen fragments with equal even-sized nonfinal payloads. Exercise the header, FCS and sixteen-member boundaries.
2. Prepare and validate fragments while the queue retains the originals. Commit the exact previews with one sequence number, allocation index and absolute deadline. Exercise cancellation, expiry, changed eligibility and replacement of a numbered source before its first local DATA transmission.
3. Account for retries at the MSDU allocation. Under IEEE Std 802.11-2024 clause 10.23.2.12.1 the frame retry count includes associated RTS failures. Under IEEE Std 802.11-2016 clauses 10.22.2.11.1 and 10.24.3, Basic uses distinct short and long counts. An accepted Basic agreement retains the allocation until its lifetime expires even when a retry counter reaches its limit. The EDCAF owns contention-window changes.
4. Admit the selected DATA/ACK or RTS/CTS/DATA/ACK exchange under the actual TXOP and TXNAV bounds. Keep the protected Basic opening preview queued through RTS where #1503 owns it. Recheck the live CTS, ownership, mode, deadline and agreement before transmission. Preserve zero-limit fragment bursts and selective A-MPDU retry.
5. Run the bounded-fragment protocol test with and without protection, the Normal Ack and Basic admission matrices, reversible preparation and recovery tests, and the existing protected Basic, fragment burst and implicit Block Ack controls. Compare relevant fingerprint, scalar and chart results with the prerequisite tree.
