A protocol-element receiver keeps the bit error of the packet

PacketReceiverBase, the base of PacketReceiver, DestreamingReceiver and StreamThroughReceiver, wrote the bit error flag of the received signal over the flag of the packet in it. A packet that carried a bit error of its own, but arrived in a signal without one, therefore lost its bit error, and the layers above accepted it. Now the flag of the signal can only add a bit error to the packet. For example, the FCS checker of LayeredEthernetInterface now discards such a frame.
