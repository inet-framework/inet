Truncated PCAP records preserve their captured bytes

The PCAP reader returns only the frame bytes present in a truncated capture record and marks them as incomplete. It skips records that captured no frame bytes and reports an FCS only when the entire FCS was captured. In PCAP 2.4 captures, replay preserves the captured bytes when the recorded original length is smaller than the captured length. Replay identifies IPv4 and IPv6 in loopback captures from either byte order.
