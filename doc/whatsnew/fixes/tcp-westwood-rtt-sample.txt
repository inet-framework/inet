TcpWestwood sets its threshold from its bandwidth estimate

TcpWestwood cleared its list of transmit times before it looked up the acknowledged segment in it, so it never took an RTT sample and never updated its bandwidth estimate. At a fast retransmission the slow-start threshold kept its initial value, and the window became 4294967295 and then wrapped to 0. TcpWestwood now takes a sample from each ACK of new data, and it sets the threshold after a loss or a timeout from its estimate, as Westwood does. A simulation with TcpWestwood gives other results.
