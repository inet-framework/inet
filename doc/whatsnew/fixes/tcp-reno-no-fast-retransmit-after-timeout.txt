After a retransmission timeout, TcpReno starts no fast retransmit in the go-back

RFC 6582 section 6 asks a sender to start no fast retransmit after a timeout before the cumulative ACK covers more than the highest sequence number that it had sent at the timeout: the go-back sends again segments that the receiver can already have, and their duplicate ACKs are no sign of a new loss. TcpNewReno, TcpTahoe and TcpWestwood had this guard, but TcpReno and DcTcp without SACK did not. Their fast retransmit in the go-back took ssthresh from the data of before the timeout, so cwnd jumped and many retransmissions left at once. Now a timeout sets recover for them too, and the duplicate ACKs below it start no fast retransmit; a loss in the go-back waits for the retransmission timer. Simulations with TcpReno without SACK and a timeout change, for example examples/inet/tcpwindowscale WS_enabled.
