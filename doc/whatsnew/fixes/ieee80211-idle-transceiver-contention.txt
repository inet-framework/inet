Wi-Fi contention works with idle transceiver radios

Wi-Fi MACs can contend when their transceiver radio is idle. During a radio mode switch, channel access waits until the radio can receive and its transmission state matches the new mode. A passive scan's queued channel change can then reach the radio even without traffic to trigger contention. With a packet-level Wi-Fi radio, the passive scan dwell starts when the radio is tuned to the requested channel and can receive.

The station also recognizes its current channel when the radio reports channel changes only on an actual retune.
