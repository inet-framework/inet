Ieee80211RadioMedium uses a default maxTransmissionDuration of 35 ms

The default maxTransmissionDuration of MediumLimitCache (10 ms) was shorter than a full-size IEEE 802.11 frame at DSSS 1 Mbit/s (12.48 ms for 1536 bytes, up to 32.95 ms for a 4095-byte HR-DSSS PSDU), causing simulations to abort with "Maximum transmission duration is exceeded" when rate control fell back to 1 Mbit/s. Ieee80211RadioMedium now overrides mediumLimitCache.maxTransmissionDuration to default(35ms). With the default transmissionPurgeMode = "timer", RadioMedium's removeNonInterferingTransmissionsTimer and signalRemoved notification occur 25 ms later, which changes event (tplx) fingerprints of Ieee80211RadioMedium simulations (215 INET example, showcase, and tutorial runs moved) while leaving link-layer (~tNl) and network-layer (~tND) trajectories unchanged.
