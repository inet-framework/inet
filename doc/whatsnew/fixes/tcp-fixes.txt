TCP fixes

Many repairs also reach the algorithms and the parameters of INET 4.7, so they change simulations with LegacyTcp too. TcpReno, TcpNewReno and DcTcp count duplicate ACKs as RFC 5681 section 2 defines them, and restart after a timeout with one full-sized segment of data. ssthresh comes from the FlightSize, and slow start grows by the acknowledged bytes, as RFC 5681 states. Each classic flavour answers an ECN-Echo, TcpTahoe answers the third duplicate ACK with slow start, and Reno and NewReno with SACK recover as RFC 6675 states; SACK no longer stops the run for flavours other than TcpReno. DCTCP marks its SYN, SYN-ACK and RST with ECT.

The receiver acknowledges at once when a segment fills a gap, and when the unacknowledged data reach 2*RMSS bytes. ECN data carry ECT(0), not ECT(1), and the echo of a CE mark is in each acknowledging segment. PAWS drops a segment with an old timestamp, and each segment after the handshake carries the timestamp option. The sender avoids silly windows, the persist timer no longer overwrites the retransmission timer, and the SYN and the SYN-ACK carry a window of at most 65535.

Nagle's algorithm now holds only new data, so it no longer holds a retransmission until the next timeout. Limited Transmit sends only unsent data. The automatic window scale represents the whole window. TcpVegas changes cwnd one time for each RTT, TcpVegas and TcpWestwood take initialSsthresh, TcpWestwood takes RTT samples and sets its threshold from its bandwidth estimate, and DumbTcp no longer sends an acknowledged FIN again.

Some runs no longer stop: a stopped Tcp drops the segments that reach it, a hard ICMP error in SYN_RCVD informs only an application that has the connection, and a SEND of zero bytes sends nothing. TcpSpoof declares the checksum of its segments correct, takes the ISS of the clock as Tcp does, and needs a destAddress, which has no default now. Parameter values without a meaning, for example an mss below 88 or a negative delayedAckTimeout, stop the run at the start with an error that names the parameter.

These repairs move fingerprints and statistical results of the INET examples, for example 27 fingerprints and 11 statistical results for the Nagle repair, and 30 fingerprints and 20 statistical results for the repairs of the parameters.
