A router sends no Router Advertisement while its link-local address is tentative

A router sent Router Advertisements from its link-local address while Duplicate Address Detection for that address was running, and before it started, although RFC 4861 Section 4.2 requires the source to be the assigned link-local address, Section 6.2.2 allows them only on an interface with an assigned unicast address, and RFC 4862 does not count a tentative address as assigned. Periodic, solicited and Proxy Mobile IPv6 advertisements are now not sent while the link-local address is tentative; a solicited advertisement that is due after the address is verified is sent as before. Simulations with short advertisement intervals, or with advertisements solicited or sent by Proxy Mobile IPv6 while a router is still verifying its address, produce different results, for example in examples/manetrouting/dymo, examples/ipv6/pmipv6 and examples/inet/hierarchical99.
