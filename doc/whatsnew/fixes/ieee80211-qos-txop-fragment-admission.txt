Wi-Fi fits Normal Ack exchanges and QoS fragments within a TXOP

Wi-Fi QoS transmitters now check the complete Normal Ack exchange against
the available TXOP before taking a queued MSDU. When a fresh grant needs
smaller fragments, the fragments share their sequence number, lifetime and
retry accounting. Protected Basic Block Ack uses its CTS reservation and
feedback rules. The fragmentation size sweep allows even nonfinal fragment
payloads when it divides an MSDU among sixteen MPDUs.
