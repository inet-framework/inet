After an ECN-Echo at a congestion window of one segment, TCP waits for the retransmission timer

RFC 3168 section 6.1.2 asks a sender whose congestion window is one segment, and so cannot halve, to reset the retransmission timer at an ECN-Echo and to send a new segment only when the timer expires. TcpReno, TcpNewReno and TcpCubic reset the timer, but sent new data at once, and they reset it also after a halving from two segments to one. Now they reset the timer only when the window is one segment at the ECN-Echo, and they send no new data until the timer expires. The new parameter eceAtOneSegment = "linux" resets no timer and holds no data, as Linux, which reduces the window as the ACKs arrive; LinuxTcp sets it. Simulations with ECN in which a sender gets an ECN-Echo at a window of one segment change, for example examples/inet/dctcp TcpRenoIncast.
