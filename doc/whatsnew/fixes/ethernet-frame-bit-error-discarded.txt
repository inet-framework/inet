Ethernet discards a frame that carries its own bit error

EthernetMacPhy and EthernetCsmaMacPhy read only the bit error flag of the received signal, and EthernetCsmaPhy wrote that flag over the flag of the frame. A frame that carried a bit error of its own, but arrived in a signal without one, therefore passed up: IPv4 answered it with an ICMP Parameter Problem, and IPv6 stopped at its assertion that the link layer drops such a frame. Now these receivers discard the frame, as they discard a frame whose signal has a bit error.
