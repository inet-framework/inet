Wi-Fi multiple-protection continuations respect the transmission opportunity limit

With Block Ack disabled, HCF now checks each additional exchange against the remaining transmission opportunity and transmitted reservation. The check includes RTS/CTS and the immediate ACK when required. A frame that cannot fit waits for another channel grant. DATA after CTS uses the mode selected when admitting its RTS.

HCF budgets the longest ACK or CTS airtime supported by the local PHY mode set. Configure multipleProtectionResponseAirtimeBound to supply a tighter bound that still covers every peer response, including the PHY overhead. Configure multipleProtectionResponseRoundTripTimeBound to cover outbound plus return propagation for each immediate ACK or CTS.
