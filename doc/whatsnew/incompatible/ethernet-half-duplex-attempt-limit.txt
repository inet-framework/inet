Half-duplex Ethernet drops a frame after 16 collisions, not 17

The two half-duplex Ethernet medium access control (MAC) modules,
EthernetCsmaMac and EthernetCsmaMacPhy, made a 17th transmission attempt
before they dropped a frame whose attempts all collided. IEEE 802.3 allows
16 (attemptLimit, clause 4.2.3.2.5 and Table 4-2), and both modules now
stop there. The backoff before each retransmission is unchanged.

This changes the results of half-duplex simulations in which a frame
collides 16 times in a row: the frame is dropped without the 17th attempt
and the backoff before it.
