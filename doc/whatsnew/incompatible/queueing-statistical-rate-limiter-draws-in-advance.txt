A statistical rate limiter draws its random number in advance

StatisticalRateLimiter drew a random number in matchesPacket(), so in pull mode each query, such as canPullPacket(), drew a number and could give another answer. It now decides with a stored random number, and it draws the next one in handlePacketFiltered() after a decision that used the stored one. The limiter draws one more random number at initialization. Simulations with a StatisticalRateLimiter therefore give other results, with the same statistical behavior.
