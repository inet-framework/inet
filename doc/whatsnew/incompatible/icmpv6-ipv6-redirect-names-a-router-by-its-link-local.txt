An IPv6 Redirect names a router by its link-local address

Ipv6NeighbourDiscovery::sendRedirect copied the next hop of the route into
the Target Address field as it was, so a route whose gateway was configured
as a global address produced a Redirect with a global Target Address. RFC
4861 sections 4.5 and 8.2 require the router's link-local address there, and
the receiving side of INET discarded every such Redirect. A router now sends
no Redirect in that case, and the Target Address is either the destination
itself or a link-local address.

Simulations with configured routes through a global gateway on a shared
link send fewer Redirects, so their fingerprints may change.
