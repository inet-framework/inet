A packet drop reason for datagrams that are too big

PacketDropReason gains PACKET_TOO_BIG, for a datagram that is larger than the MTU of the next link and may not be fragmented, with the result filter packetDropReasonIsPacketTooBig. Ipv4 reports it for a datagram with the Don't Fragment flag set, and Mpls for a labeled datagram with that flag; both reported OTHER_PACKET_DROP before. Ipv4 records the new statistic packetDropPacketTooBig.

Ipv4's packetDropUndefined no longer counts these drops, so its results change in simulations where routers drop such datagrams, for example examples/inet/tcp_pmtud and examples/quic/dplpmtud. A PacketDropVisualizer label that shows the reason now shows 16 or PACKET_TOO_BIG instead of -1 or OTHER_PACKET_DROP.
