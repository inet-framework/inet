IPv6 fills in two length fields it used to leave wrong

A Packet Too Big report carried an MTU of zero. Icmpv6::sendErrorMessage took no MTU and handed createPacketTooBigMsg a literal 0, under a bare "TODO implement MTU support". It takes one now, and Ipv6 passes the MTU it has already read from the outgoing interface two lines above the call, as RFC 4443 section 3.2 requires. A source that receives the report can act on it.

Every fragment of a fragmented datagram carried the Payload Length of the whole datagram, because Ipv6::fragmentAndSend builds each fragment from a copy of the base header. Each fragment now carries its own: the fragment header plus that fragment's share.

Simulations that fragment IPv6 datagrams or report a too-big packet put different bytes on the wire, so their fingerprints may change.
