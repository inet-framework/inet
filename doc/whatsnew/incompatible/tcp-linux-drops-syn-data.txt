LinuxTcp drops the data of a SYN without Fast Open

A TCP listener keeps the data of a SYN that carries no Fast Open option and delivers it after the handshake, as RFC 9293 section 3.10.7.2 allows. A Linux listener drops such data, and the client sends it again after the SYN-ACK. The new parameter dropSynData selects the behavior of Linux; LinuxTcp sets it, and Tcp keeps the data. Simulations with LinuxTcp in which a client sends data on a SYN without Fast Open change. The data of a SYN with an invalid Fast Open cookie is dropped in each mode, as RFC 7413 section 4.2.2 asks, as before.
