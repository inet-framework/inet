A mobile node holds home-address traffic until its reverse tunnel exists

A Mobile IPv6 mobile node that had just moved to a foreign network
discarded the packets it sent from its home address until the home agent
acknowledged its registration ("Using HoA instead of CoA... dropping
datagram"). It now holds them, at most 100 per home agent, and sends them
through the reverse tunnel once the tunnel exists; held packets are dropped
with a packetDropped signal if the registration does not complete. A held
Home Test Init is not retransmitted while it waits, so a late
acknowledgement does not make return routability run twice. In
examples/ipv6/mipv6roaming this recovers a Home Test Init and two echo
replies in run 0. Fingerprints of the Handover and Roaming configurations
change.
