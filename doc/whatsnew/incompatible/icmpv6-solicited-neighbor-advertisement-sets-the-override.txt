A solicited Neighbor Advertisement sets the Override flag

Internet Protocol version 6 (IPv6) Neighbour Discovery decided the
Override flag of a Neighbor Advertisement sent in reply to a Neighbor
Solicitation from the wrong option, so the replies to Neighbour Unreachability Detection probes and to
Duplicate Address Detection probes went out with the flag clear. With the
flag clear, a receiver keeps its cached link-layer address when the
advertisement carries a different one (Request for Comments (RFC) 4861
Section 7.2.5). The flag is now set, as RFC 4861 Section 7.2.4 specifies. Fingerprints that hash
message contents or packet bytes of IPv6 networks in which such probes are
answered change.
