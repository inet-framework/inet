Error rates of signal regions

IErrorModel has two new pure virtual functions: a second computePacketErrorRate() and a second computeBitErrorRate() give the error rates of a region of the header part or the data part, that is, a number of content bits that the transmitter sends in a time interval. The rates use the SNIR of that interval. The regions of a part share the bits that the rate of the part counts, so at a constant SNIR the rates of the regions of the header and the data give the rate of the whole signal. StochasticErrorModel, ApskErrorModel, Ieee802154ErrorModel, the IEEE 802.11 NIST and Yans models, and Ieee80211BerTableErrorModel give region rates. ErrorModelBase computes the region rate from the bit error rate of the region, so a model that derives from it adds only the second computeBitErrorRate(). An IErrorModel implementation outside INET must add both functions.
