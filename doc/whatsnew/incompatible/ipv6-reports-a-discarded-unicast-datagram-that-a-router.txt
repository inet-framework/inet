IPv6 reports a discarded unicast datagram that a router answers with an error

An IPv6 router that discarded a forwarded unicast datagram and answered it
with an Internet Control Message Protocol for IPv6 (ICMPv6) error emitted
no packetDropped signal. This affected a datagram with no route to its
destination, one whose hop limit ran out, and one too large for the next
link. PacketDropVisualizer drew nothing for these drops, and no listener on
the signal saw them. The signal is now emitted, with the reasons
NO_ROUTE_FOUND, HOP_LIMIT_REACHED and OTHER_PACKET_DROP, as IPv4 does at
the same places.
A listener that counts packetDropped from Ipv6 sees these new emissions.
No fingerprint or statistic changes.
