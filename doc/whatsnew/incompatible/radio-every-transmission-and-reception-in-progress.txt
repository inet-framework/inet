Every transmission and reception in progress of a radio

IRadio gained getTransmissionsInProgress() and getReceptionsInProgress(), which return every transmission the radio is transmitting and every reception it is attempting, in no particular order. ReceiverBase and the canvas visualizers use them instead of getTransmissionInProgress() and getReceptionInProgress(), so that they also work for a radio that has several signals in progress at once (see the next item). For Radio, NoiseSource and ShortcutRadio, which have at most one in each direction, the vectors hold at most one element, and nothing else changes.

This change requires the modification of C++ code that implements IRadio without deriving from Radio: the two methods are pure virtual.
