Custom IEEE 802.11 policies for protected Basic Block Ack exchanges

Custom A-MSDU aggregation policies must accept a complete-MPDU fit predicate. Custom QoS acknowledgment policies must provide length-based eligibility and feedback prediction. Custom QoS originator data services must support reversible size-limited preparation and commitment of the resulting MPDU or fragments. See “IEEE 802.11 Basic Block Ack policy interfaces” in the migration guide for the affected overrides.
