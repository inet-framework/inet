IEEE 802.11 QoS DATA lifetime

HCF now discards QoS DATA when its finite lifetime expires, with Block Ack support enabled or disabled. Each access category uses mac.hcf.edca.edcaf[*].msduLifetime, with a default of 0.512 seconds. The timer starts at MAC acceptance, so DATA can expire in the queue before transmission. Users can select another positive, finite lifetime for congestion and compatibility studies. A-MSDU construction restarts the aggregate timer; fragmentation, retransmission, and ACK-policy changes preserve the applicable deadline. Accepted exchanges retain their DATA until safe completion and permit no later attempt after expiration.

Congested QoS simulations can transmit newer DATA because older queued or in-progress DATA expires. Recorded trajectories change in the Block Ack fragmentation showcase, the QoS showcase, and the wireless and ad hoc MacQos examples. The approved fingerprint baselines reflect these lifetime changes.

HCF records lifetime drops separately in packetDropLifetimeExpired. Its count and byte sum separate lifetime disposal from queue overflow and retry-limit disposal. Aggregation with qOsCheck=false still accepts non-QoS or mixed sources. The resulting QoS aggregate starts its own lifetime at construction, even when its sources have no lifetime entries.
