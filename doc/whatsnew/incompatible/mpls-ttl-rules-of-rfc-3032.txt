MPLS follows the TTL rules of RFC 3032

The TTL field of every label stack entry was 0. A pushed entry now copies the TTL of the entry below it, or of the IPv4 header when it is the first label, and each LSR forwards a labeled packet with the incoming TTL minus one, as RFC 3032 section 2.4 and the Uniform Model of RFC 3443 state.

The IPv4 TTL also counts the LSRs now. The ingress decrements it before it labels a datagram, and leaves a datagram with TTL 1 to IPv4, which reports it; a pop that empties the label stack writes the outgoing TTL into the IPv4 header. Before, a datagram left an LSP with the TTL it had at the ingress. An LSR now discards a labeled packet whose outgoing TTL is zero, with a packetDropped signal; before, no LSR looked at the TTL.
