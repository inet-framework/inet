IEEE 802.11 recipient responses do not extend their local NAV

Tx requires an explicit local NAV choice in both transmitFrame() overloads. The NAV records a reservation of the medium. Custom C++ implementations and callers must add updateLocalNav. DCF holder frames use true. HCF holder frames and recipient responses use false. The migration guide supplies the signatures and completion rules.

CTS, ACK, and Basic Block Ack responses retain their duration fields without extending the recipient's local NAV. Received reservations still control channel access. For example, a recipient can answer another RTS from the same holder when its own previous CTS would otherwise leave the medium busy.

HCF clamps response durations to zero and applies ceiling to whole microseconds before transmission. These shared changes can change Wi-Fi simulation results.
