Interference for a listening of the caller's making

IRadioMedium gained computeInterference(receiver, listening), formerly a protected member of RadioMedium: the background noise plus the receptions, at the receiver, of the transmissions on the medium that overlap the listening, for a listening that need not belong to any transmission. The result is not cached, but the receptions in it come from the medium's reception cache: the ones not computed yet are computed at the call, and draw their random numbers, if any, then. Together with the propagation, the receiver's createListening() and the analog model, it lets a radio compute a hypothetical SNIR (e.g. for link-quality estimation) from public API.

This change requires the modification of C++ code that implements IRadioMedium without deriving from RadioMedium.
