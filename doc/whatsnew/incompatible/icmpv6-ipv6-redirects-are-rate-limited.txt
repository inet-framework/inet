IPv6 Redirects are rate limited

A router sent one Internet Control Message Protocol for IPv6 (ICMPv6)
Redirect for every datagram that qualified, with no limit, although RFC
4861 section 8.2 requires one. Ipv6NeighbourDiscovery now
meters them with the token bucket that RFC 4443 section 2.4 (f) recommends.
The two new parameters redirectRate (default 10Hz) and redirectBurst (default
10) are the rate N and the burst size B of that bucket, and the defaults are
the example values of that section.

Simulations where a source keeps sending through a router that redirects it
send fewer Redirects, so their fingerprints may change.
