RIP and RIPng corrections

A RIPng message now carries the version 1 of RFC 2080; before, every RIPng message carried the version 2 of RIP.

A RIPng answer to a request now leaves on the interface that the request came from, from the link-local address of that interface (RFC 2080 section 2.5). Before, an answer to the link-local address of a requester left on the first interface of the router, and was lost; so trajectories of RIPng simulations with requests change.
