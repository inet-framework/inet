The SNIR of a time interval

ISnir has three new pure virtual functions: getMin(startTime, endTime), getMax(startTime, endTime), and getMean(startTime, endTime). They return the SNIR of a time interval inside the reception; an interval of one point of time gives the SNIR at that time. A model of a part of the signal, for example of one chunk of the data, can thus use the SNIR of the time of that part. ScalarSnir, DimensionalSnir, and UnitDiskSnir implement them, and the whole-reception values do not change. An ISnir implementation outside INET must add the three functions.

SnirReceiverBase has a new overload of computeIsReceptionSuccessful() with a time interval, which checks the SNIR threshold for that interval. The function without the interval calls it with the limits of the reception, so its results do not change.
