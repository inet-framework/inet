MPLS works on Ethernet links

An MPLS router with an Ethernet interface stopped the simulation at the first ARP packet, because the MPLS module threw on every protocol other than MPLS and IPv4. Such a packet now goes to the network layer. A labeled packet now also goes through the Ethernet encapsulation, with the ethertype 8847 hex. A LIB entry holds no next hop, so the frame goes to the broadcast address of the link; on a point-to-point link that is the one neighbor.
