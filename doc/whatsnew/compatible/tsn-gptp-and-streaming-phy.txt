gPTP and TSN devices: all messages on the wire, the Follow_Up after the Sync, and the streaming PHY

The gPTP serializer now writes and reads the Announce message and the originTimestamp of a one-step Sync; before, it threw "unknown GptpMessageType" and "GptpSync without twoStepFlag unimplemented". The reserved octets of Pdelay_Req round-trip.

gPTP now sends the Follow_Up when the Sync is completely transmitted, not when its transmission starts, and it takes the packets from the transmissionEnded signals. So gPTP and cut-through switching can be on together; before, the run stopped with "Returning an incomplete chunk is not allowed" (issue #1100). The fingerprints of gPTP simulations change, but the accuracy of the synchronization does not.

TsnSwitch and TsnDevice use EthernetStreamingPhyLayer when hasTimeSynchronization is true, and EthernetStreamThroughPhyLayer when hasCutthroughSwitching is true; TsnClock always uses EthernetStreamingPhyLayer. A model no longer sets the typename of the transmitter and the receiver for gPTP itself.
