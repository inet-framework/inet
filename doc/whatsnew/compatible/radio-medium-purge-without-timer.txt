Radio medium purge without a timer

RadioMedium has a new transmissionPurgeMode parameter. The default, "timer", removes transmissions that can no longer interfere with a self-message scheduled at the interference end time of the oldest transmission that can still interfere, as before. "onTransmission" removes them whenever a new transmission is added instead, so the medium schedules no events of its own; this keeps the medium out of the event sequence entirely, which matters for a model whose fingerprints must not depend on the medium's bookkeeping. Interference is computed identically in both modes.

The protected removeNonInterferingTransmissions() of RadioMedium now only removes; it no longer reschedules the timer, which the timer handler does by calling the new scheduleRemoveNonInterferingTransmissionsTimer() after it. A subclass that calls or overrides removeNonInterferingTransmissions() and relied on the rescheduling must call that method itself.
