Documentation: the website from the repository, and new chapters for IEEE 802.11 and Ethernet bridging

The website inet.omnetpp.org and the documentation of the versions that doc/src/versions.toml lists are now built with Sphinx from this repository. doc/src/build builds them in the documentation builder image of OMNeT++ (with the options --serve, --only, --versions and --pdf), and the site workflow publishes them. The NED reference comes from opp_docgen. This build replaces the old Jekyll and opp_neddoc build, and .readthedocs.yaml is removed.

The IEEE 802.11 chapters of the User's Guide and of the Developer's Guide are rewritten. The Developer's Guide gives a map of the architecture by layer and plane, figures of the interfaces, of the MAC and of the coordination functions, and a catalog of the parts. The User's Guide follows the functions of the MAC, and it describes HT, multiple protection, the mapped 5 GHz band and the limits of the model. The Ethernet chapter of the User's Guide has new sections about the architecture of a bridge (EthernetSwitch, BridgingLayer, the MAC address table, STP, RSTP and MRP), VLANs, provider bridging (IEEE 802.1ad) and priority. The User's Guide describes Radiotap capture, and the Testing chapter of the Developer's Guide describes the fingerprint test with opp_repl and code coverage.

About 30 showcase and tutorial pages are corrected, so that their text agrees with the model: the names of parameters, configurations and modules, the defaults, the keys of the visualizers, and the links to the source files of moved showcases. tutorials/fingerprint teaches the fingerprint test with opp_repl and the fingerprint store.
