On-demand path discovery in native IEEE 802.11 mesh interfaces

Ieee80211MeshInterface now selects HWMP by default. Unresolved native unicast traffic waits in a bounded shared source queue while PREQ/PREP exchanges discover paths using the measured directional airtime costs. Route freshness, lifetimes, precursor admission and packet deadlines govern release; group forwarding remains immediate. The initial profile supports open OFDM meshes without aggregation or power save. Path-error and proactive-root procedures are separate additions.

Queue count, bytes and wait time, discovery retry policy and the finite received-PREQ identity history are configurable. Isolated forwarding and HWMP owner restarts clear volatile state while preserving their node-owned sequence identities and the required per-target PREQ pacing history.

Stopped mesh management also discards late MAC packet deliveries through its normal management drop path. This allows the management owner to stop independently while the MAC is still active; unexpected non-packet inputs and self-messages retain the base lifecycle diagnostics.
