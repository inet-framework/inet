Vector recovery for replicated streams (IEEE 802.1CB)

The new VectorStreamMerger is a merger for replicated streams with the vector recovery algorithm of IEEE 802.1CB-2017 and the correction of the forward window boundary of IEEE 802.1CBdb-2021. It keeps a history of sequence positions, compares sequence numbers modulo 2^16, rejects duplicate and rogue packets, has reset timeouts, can accept packets without a sequence number tag, and recovers each member stream. Select it in place of StreamMerger with the typename of the merger. StreamMerger and its defaults do not change.

The algorithm is also available alone as VectorSequenceRecovery, without packet handling and timers: the caller gives the packets and schedules the timeouts.

The module needs complete frames, and it does not give latent error detection, MRA or the standard management interfaces. Unit, module and protocol tests and a showcase of failures and of sequence number wraparound come with it.

The vector recovery was contributed by Afshin Zanganeh.
