Configurable vector recovery for replicated streams

VectorStreamMerger adds sequence-position history, modular sequence comparison, duplicate and rogue rejection, reset timeouts, optional tagless acceptance and individual member-stream recovery. It implements the documented IEEE 802.1CB-2017 base vector-recovery behavior with the IEEE 802.1CBdb-2021 forward-window boundary correction. Select it in place of StreamMerger through the merger typename parameter. Existing StreamMerger behavior and defaults are unchanged.

The module requires complete frames and does not provide latent error detection, MRA or the standard management interfaces. Unit, module and protocol tests and a failure/wraparound showcase accompany the implementation.
