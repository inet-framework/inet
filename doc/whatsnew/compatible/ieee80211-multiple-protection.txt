IEEE 802.11 selectable multiple protection in EDCA TXOPs

EDCA TXOPs support selectable multiple protection. Single protection remains the default. Nominal timing uses the mode set's short interframe space (SIFS) unchanged after the first transmission step. The first step adds no interframe space. Multiple protection stops normally when another exchange cannot use a legal duration field.

For example, a 200 us PPDU within a 1024 us TXOP advertises 824 us of reservation. A TXOP is a transmission opportunity; a PPDU is one PHY transmission.

The user guide explains nominal timing, zero-limit estimates, unused reservation time, and support limits. Multiple protection uses shared TXNAV. It does not check a complete exchange against the remaining budget.
