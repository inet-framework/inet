MQTT-SN clients and an MQTT gateway over UDP

The new MqttSnClient and MqttSnGateway applications model MQTT-SN v1.2 over UDP, including topic registration, QoS -1/0/1/2, Wills, keepalive, sleeping-client buffering and IPv4 gateway discovery. The gateway connects to the existing MQTT 3.1.1 broker over TCP and terminates each hop's QoS exchange independently. Configured IPv6 unicast is supported. Wired and wireless IP examples and a user-guide chapter explain configuration and the limits of in-memory sessions; native sensor-link transports, encryption and energy modeling are outside this profile.
