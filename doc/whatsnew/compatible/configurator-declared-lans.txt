The network configurators accept LANs that wires do not show

A new <lan> element of the Ipv4NetworkConfigurator and Ipv6NetworkConfigurator configuration declares a LAN that the configurators cannot find by following wires: interfaces joined by a path that is not a wire (for example a cellular NIC whose frames reach a bridge through a tunnel), and the ports of a bridge inside a node that routes on its other interfaces, when the bridge has an interface table of its own. Its <interface> child elements select the members, and their interfaceTableModule attribute names the interface table of a bridge's ports. The members and the wired LANs behind them get one subnet, and routes as on any LAN:

  <lan id="sessions">
    <interface hosts="ue[*]" names="cellular"/>
    <interface hosts="upf" interfaceTableModule="ethBridge.interfaceTable"/>
  </lan>
